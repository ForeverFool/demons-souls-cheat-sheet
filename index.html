  • If you have Pure Black World Tendency, Rydell will appear as a Black Phantom in front of his cell. He drops a Phosphorescent Pole

  • If your World Tendency is just above Pure Black, there will be a Primeval Demon that will spawn here that may drop a Colorless Demon's Soul
    • 5-1
  • If you have Pure White World Tendency, there will be a ladder halfway across the walkway with the first Giant Depraved One. Climb the ladder and make your way down the path to find three Giant Depraved Ones guarding the Istarelle
    • 5-2

  • If you have Pure White World Tendency, Selen Vinland will appear on an island near the start of the walkway. Talk to her to begin her questline. Once you collect the Crest of Vinland from Black Phantom Garl Vinland in the Dirty Colossus Archstone (requires Pure White World Tendency), give it to her to receive a Ring of Devout Prayer if you have 10 or more Faith

    Tutorial

    • From the start head down the passages and corridors past the Dreglings to some stairs to grab Crescent Moon Grass
    • Continue to a bridge and on your left will be Crescent Moon Grass x3
    • Drop down into the marshy area and continue to a gazebo-like structure to find an Archstone. Touch with it to be taken to the next area
    • Continue forward through the halls and past the Blue Eye Knights to your left behind some barrels is Half Moon Grass
    • Climb the stairs and on the left will be an Unknown Soldier's Soul
    • Take the right path and climb the stairs past the forge. Pass the fog gate and take a right down some stairs to grab Half Moon Grass x2
    • Turn around and head back up the stairs and continue up the stairs to another fog gate you can cross. Defeat Vanguard; acquire Grey Demon's Soul
    • Touch with the Archstone to be taken to the next area
    • At the end of the corridor before the stairs you can grab Full Moon Grass
    • Facing the dragon, take the left stairs to grab Full Moon Grass
    • Take a left behind you and to your right behind a pillar is Full Moon Grass x2
    • Turn around to grab Shard of Hardstone x3
    • In the next room is Shard of Sharpstone x3
    • Next is a Renowned Soldier's Soul
    • And another Renowned Soldier's Soul
    • And one more Renowned Soldier's Soul
    • Continue forward to be sucker-punched into the Nexus

    The Nexus

    • If you died to Vanguard you will be taken here
    • Speak to the Crestfallen Warrior
    • Talk to Stockpile Thomas
    • Speak to Blacksmith Boldwin
    • Talk to the Worshipper of God
      • If you have at least 20 Faith, she will give you a Pure Faintstone + Stone of Ephemeral Eyes x2
    • Head up the circular stairs to the very top of the Nexus, then turn to face the giant statue in the middle. You can carefully descend the block-like ledges down on the right to a corpse with a Renowned Soldier's Soul
    • Once again, head up to the top of the Nexus. This time, descend the opposite side to find a corpse with New Moon Grass x2
    • Touch the Archstone of the Small King and warp to the Boletarian Palace

    Boletarian Palace

    • Upon being transported, turn around and behind planks you will find Late Moon Grass x2
    • Go the other way and grab the Crescent Moon Grass x3 before the stairs and the Unknown Soldier's Soul a few steps further
    • Continue up the stairs and take the left path. Loot Half Moon Grass 2x atop the wooden scaffold with an archer
    • After the next room and to the right behind breakable shelves will be Firebomb x5
    • A little further will be a fog gate. Pass through it and to your right will be an Unknown Soldier's Soul and a bit further will be a Mail Breaker
    • Continue upward to some scaffolding then take a left to end up outside. At the top you can take a left through a doorway. Take a left to grab Half Moon Grass x2
    • Go up the stairs where the trap is. Where it breaks part of the scaffolding will be a Bastard Sword
    • Head up the stairs to a rampart. Take the path behind you and at the end you will find Half Moon Grass x4
    • Enter the tower and take a left out onto another rampart with a dangerous Red Eye Knight guarding an Unknown Hero's Soul
    • Go back across the previous rampart to a Blue Eye Knight by a fog gate. Opposite the fog gate, drop down the broken section of wall to a corpse with a Light Crossbow and Wooden Bolt x16
    • Climb back up to the rampart again and head straight to enter a tower with Firebomb x6
    • Head down the stairs here and take a left eventually to find a balcony with two chains you can break. Continue down the tower to find a lever you can pull to open a shortcut and grab the Cling Ring
    • To your right outside you can grab the Old Raggedy Set + Old Spice x2
    • Here you can also grab the Jade Hair Ornament
    • Climb back up the tower and onto the rampart and cross the fog gate on your right. Take a right down some stairs and take a left. Take a right then another right and destroy some wooden planks to reach a room with the Dregling Merchant
    • Head back to the base of the stairs we just descended and parallel to them you can walk over the wall to an area with the Thief's Ring
    • Talk to Ostrava, of Boletaria. He will ask you to kill the Dreglings just below you. Do so and he will jump down afterwards and you may talk to him to receive the Brass Telescope. NOTE: Ostrava will also patrol around the nearby area, so make sure he doesn't get himself killed
    • Grab the Unknown Soldier's Soul from here as well
    • Continue forward and take a right from the tower where the Boletaria Soldiers jump from. To your left in the side corridor is a Scimitar
    • Continue to the right into an armory. Head down the wooden stairs and grab Shard of Hardstone x3 + Shard of Sharpstone x3
    • Head back up the wooden stairs and behind the small left corner will be an Unknown Hero's Soul
    • Go back and head into the hallway past the Blue Eye Knight. To your left will be an Unknown Soldier's Soul
    • Head forward and take a right to break through some shelves. Head up the stairs here and take a right up some more stairs. Take a right at the top onto some scaffolding to pick up an Unknown Soldier's Soul
    • Continue up the stone stairs and to your left on some scaffolding will be Late Moon Grass x2
    • Turn around and pass through the fog gate. Continue forward and take a right past the trap. At the end take a right up some stairs and continue forward across the rampart where the Red Dragon attacks. To your right at the end you can pick up an Unknown Soldier's Soul
    • Enter the nearby door and use the lever to open the main gate to the Palace
    • Turn around and head forward outside. Take a left and break through some shelves to reach Turpentine x4 at the top of the stairs
    • Turn back around and head down the stairs. Take a right then a left then another right into a room with some tables. Head down the stairs past the Hoplite and take a right outside to grab an Unknown Hero's Soul
    • Continue down the tower and at the bottom you can pick up Half Moon Grass x3
    • Pull the lever to open the shortcut
    • Head outside and to your right will be a fog gate leading to the boss fight. Defeat Phalanx; acquire Lead Demon's Soul
    • Touch the Archstone to return to the Nexus
    • Talk to the Crestfallen Warrior
    • Speak to the Maiden in Black
    • Speak to Stockpile Thomas and give him the Jade Hair Ornament for a Ring of Herculean Strength
    • Talk to the Worshipper of God
    • Speak to the Disciple of God, who can teach you Miracles if you have at least 10 Faith
    • Talk to the Acolyte of God
    • Head to the opposite side of the Nexus to speak to Freke's Apprentice, who can teach you Spells if you have at least 10 Magic
    • Head up five sets of stairs and on a walkway you can talk to the Monumental. Accept the mission
    • Continue to the stairs to the right of the Monumental and head down. At the base of the stairs on your left will be a Stone of Ephemeral Eyes
    • If you are online, you can continue to the top of the Nexus to enter the Pantheon. At the end of the second curving hallway on the left will be a Stone of Ephemeral Eyes
    • Return to the bottom of the nexus to talk to the Maiden in Black and she will give you the Blue Eye Stone + White Eye Stone. Talk to her again and agree to 'Seek Soul Power'. She will allow you to level up via Souls
    • Talk to the Crestfallen Warrior again
    • Climb the stairs to a high spot and jump off the edge to commit suicide and return to Soul Form
    • Speak to the Crestfallen Warrior once more

    Stonefang Tunnel

    • Touch the Archstone of the Burrow King and warp to Stonefang Tunnel
    • Upon being transported, take the stairs up and head left to quickly kill a Crystal Lizard for upgrade materials
    • Continue forward to grab at the ledge an Unknown Hero's Soul
    • Back near the top of the stairs by the Archstone is the Filthy Man
    • Cross the nearby bridge to the open area where enemies are throwing boulders. In the left corner behind a cart is Sticky White Stuff x2
    • Head into the tower and to the right will be a lever you can turn. Head out the doorway here and hop onto the lift going upward. Hop off onto the next platform with a doorway. Enter and on a balcony behind the Fat Official is a Crushing Battle Axe+1
    • In the room is also a Renowned Soldier's Soul
    • Head back outside and take the lift down to pick up a Stone of Ephemeral Eyes
    • Hop back on the lift and drop off onto the first platform that leads back to where we activated the lift. Continue through the hallway to the next room full of miners, where one holding a bag is guaranteed to drop Shard of Hardstone x4 + Shard of Sharpstone x4
    • Break through the pots to enter a room with a Fat Official. Behind him will be another room with a Chunk of Hardstone + Chunk of Sharpstone
    • Pick up Shard of Clearstone x4 from here as well
    • Turn around and head back into the previous room. To the side will be a small doorway leading to a tunnel. Climb the stairs here to pick up Crescent Moon Grass x4 at the corner
    • Continue forward and to your right will be a room with a Pickaxe
    • Head out of the tunnel to a wooden walkway and take a left. Climb the stone stairs and on a corpse near a flaming cornerstone will be an Unknown Soldier's Soul
    • Go back down the stone stairs and stick to the left side of the wooden bridge to avoid falling when it breaks. Cross to the other side with the Fat Official and grab a Renowned Hero's Soul
    • Drop down to the stone plateau and pull the lever to open a gate
    • Beside the lever is a bag-holding miner that will drop Shard of Clearstone x2 + Chunk of Clearstone
    • Another nearby bag-holding miner will drop Shard of Hardstone x4 + Large Hardstone Shard
    • In the far corner of this platform behind some wooden clutter is a corpse with Ed's Grindstone x2
    • Cross the nearby wooden bridge and continue across the stone walkway to kill yet another bag-holding miner that will drop Shard of Sharpstone x4 + Large Sharpstone Shard
    • Loot the nearby corpse on the edge for an Unknown Soldier's Soul
    • Drop down again and head through the tunnel. Go up the stone stairs and cross the stone walkway to the gate we opened. Pass through the fog gate. Take a right past the Fire Lizard to grab a Shard of Meltstone
    • Head up the wooden ramp and take a left. Head up a wooden ramp and at the top you can pick up Half Moon Grass x2
    • Press on and take a right into a circular room with Fire Lizards and a Shard of Dragonstone
    • Exit and continue forward. Ignore the fog gate on your left and break through the wooden clutter. There will be two bag-holding miners here, one of which will drop Shard of Hardstone x9 and the other will drop Large Hardstone Shard x2 + Large Sharpstone Shard
    • Continue outside and to your left on the ledge will be an Unknown Hero's Soul
    • Turn around and next to the bridge will be Crescent Moon Grass x2
    • Head across the wooden platform and turn the lever to start the lift
    • Halfway down on a wooden platform will be a Shard of Clearstone
    • Take the lift to the bottom and get off. Take the door outside to a wooden platform with a Renowned Hero's Soul
    • Go back inside and talk to Blacksmith Ed. He will allow you to make Weapons and various items from Demon Souls
    • Take the lift all the way back up and head back to the sloping tunnel. Take the fog gate on your right and head down the short tunnel to reach a forge-like area. On your left behind a furnace will be a Large Hardstone Shard
    • As soon as you exit the forge room, turn left and quickly kill the Crystal Lizard around the corner for upgrade materials
    • Starting from the door to the forge room, go down some wooden stairs and sprint across the wooden board to reach an area with two Unknown Soldier's Souls and a Great Club. Note that if you fail to cross the board before it breaks you will have to reload the area to reach the items again
    • Drop down onto the narrow ledge on your left then onto the floor. Head up the wooden ramps and make your way through the fog gate tunnel and out to the hall. Take a right and roll through several barrels and climb the stairs behind them. Up here you can grab Crescent Moon Grass x2
    • At the end corner on your right will be a ledge you can drop onto to pick up an Unknown Hero's Soul
    • Drop down onto the crates to grab a Steel Shield
    • Run across the wooden bridge here and go up the stairs. Continue all the way to the large stone with Fire Lizards. Turn left to grab a Shard of Dragonstone
    • Turn right here and take a left down the ramp. Around the corner to your left will be Ed's Grindstone x2
    • Turn around and head to the bridge past the ramp we just descended. Down the first ramp will be Half Moon Grass
    • Continue down and take a left behind you. On your left you can pick up an Unknown Hero's Soul under the ramp
    • Also here is a bag-holding miner that will drop Shard of Sharpstone x4 + Large Sharpstone Shard + Shard of Clearstone x2
    • Just ahead is a lever you can turn to stop the waterwheel and open a path ahead
    • Head forward and drop down. Take a right and head up the wooden ramp. Head past the circular room with Fire Lizards and drop down on the right to pick up a Ring of Poison Resistance
    • Turn around and head through the busted cell bars. Here you can pick up Half Moon Grass x4 and a Large Sharpstone Shard
    • Head up the stairs where the Grass was and go all the way to the buckets. Take a right and go down the stairs to a tunnel with several explosive barrels. Head through the fog gate behind the Fat Official and to your right behind the wagons you can grab a Stone of Ephemeral Eyes
    • Head across the wooden walkway and head up the wooden ramp. Take a left and head up the wooden ramp to your left. Take a left and jump across the gap to reach a Kris Blade
    • Drop down and facing the square platform with a lever, check the far right corner to find a well-hidden Shard of Spiderstone x2
    • Return to the square platform and turn the lever to lower it downwards
    • Facing the fog gate, behind you to your left is a tunnel where you can pick up a Chunk of Spiderstone
    • Go through the fog gate. Defeat Armor Spider; acquire Hard Demon's Soul + Pure Spiderstone
    • Around the boss room you can also pick up a Renowned Soldier's Soul and a Chunk of Spiderstone
    • Touch the Archstone to return to the Nexus and take care of any business
    • Speak to the Crestfallen Warrior
    • Talk to Blacksmith Boldwin
    • Speak to the Worshipper of God
    • Talk to Freke's Apprentice
    • Return to Stonefang Tunnel and talk to Blacksmith Ed
    • Suicide here in Stonefang Tunnel to return to Soul Form

    Tower of Latria

    • Touch the Archstone of the Tower Queen to be taken to the Tower of Latria
    • Head out onto the walkway and take a right to find Augite of Guidance x3 in the last cell
    • Turn around and head down the walkway. Take a right and on the wall is the Prison of Hope, 3F Cell Key
    • To the left of the key behind the coffins is a Renowned Soldier's Soul
    • Use the key to unlock the first cell behind you and pick up the Wizard's Set + Wooden Catalyst
    • Head right down the walkway and at the end behind the coffins is a Renowned Soldier's Soul
    • To your left in the cell is a Shard of Mercurystone
    • Head back down the walkway and to your right will be a corridor. Turn right at the end and open the first cell to pick up a Shard of Mercurystone
    • Turn around and go the other way across the walkway. In the second cell after the Dregling in the corner will be a Silver Coronet
    • Continue down the walkway and on the right on the wall will be Prison of Hope, 3F Key
    • Continue along this side of the walkway and in the third cell on the left will be a Shard of Mercurystone
    • At the end of this walkway is a Renowned Soldier's Soul
    • Head all the way back to the walkway with the Archstone and at the end is a locked gate you can now open
    • Pass through the fog gate. Head up the stairs and open the first cell on your left. Here you can open the coffin to pick up a Stone of Ephemeral Eyes
    • Just behind that is an Unknown Hero's Soul
    • Continue until you reach the opposite walkway. Open the first cell on your left to grab Fresh Spice
    • Continue down the walkway and in the last cell is a coffin on your left you can open to find the Assassin's Set
    • Open the coffin on your right to find an Unknown Hero's Soul
    • Head out onto the walkway and on your right on the wall will be Prison of Hope, 4F Key + Prison of Hope, 4F Cell Key
    • Head back to the previous hall and head to the walkway on the other side. Open the first cell on your right to find an Unknown Hero's Soul, a Secret Dagger, and Claws
    • Go back out and raise the gate on your left. Here you can grab Fresh Spice x3 from the corpse by the coffins
    • On your right a bit ahead is another gate you can raise. After that open the cell on your right to find the Once Royal Mistress
    • Head back out and on your right you can pick up a Renowned Hero's Soul
    • Go around to the other side and at the end of the walkway in the cell on your left will be three coffins containing a Ring of Magical Sharpness and two Unknown Hero's Souls
    • Lift the gate and head down the stairs. Just before the walkway you can pick up Old Spice
    • In the third cell on the right, you can speak to Sage Freke, the Visionary, who we will free in a bit
    • Go back into the stairwell and continue down. In the hall on your right you can pick up the Prison of Hope, 1F Key + Prison of Hope, 1F Cell Key
    • Continue forward down the stairs and exit out onto the first walkway you see. Grab a Heavy Crossbow + Black Bolt x9
    • Continue on and in the last cell on the left, you can speak to Lord Rydell
    • Backtrack across the walkway and go down the stairs to open the gate at the bottom. Head out onto the walkway and open the second door on your left to pick up an Unknown Soldier's Soul
    • Continue to the end of the walkway to pick up the Silver Bracelets and the Fluted Set + Kite Shield
    • Open the first door on your left as you head on to pick up an Unknown Soldier's Soul
    • At the end of the walkway by the Prisoner Horde will be Old Spice x3
    • Continue to the next hall and take a left. On your left in the third cell will be an Unknown Soldier's Soul
    • At the end of the walkway behind some coffins will be an open door. Head outside and go down the stairs to your left to pick up a Shard of Moonlightstone
    • Turn around and head forward until you can take a right, while still staying outside. At the end of the outside walkway among some rubble will be an Estoc
    • Back inside, circle around to the opposite walkway and quickly kill the Crystal Lizard for upgrade materials
    • In the first cell of this walkway is also an Unknown Soldier's Soul
    • By the open double gates will be a gate you can open
    • Head through the double gates and head head right. To your left through some coffins will be a broken fence you can walk through. Carefully head along the ledge here to the end to grab a Stone of Ephemeral Eyes
    • Go back to the walkway and this time head in the opposite direction. On your right at the end here will be another broken fence you can walk through onto a ledge. Head left and at the end of the ledge will be a Mercury Rapier+1
    • Head back up to the main walkway and continue to the start of the walkway with the Ballista. Grab the Prison of Hope, Warden's Key, which will be the first item you can grab here
    • Go all the way back to the stairwell with the two gates that we couldn't open earlier. Open the first gate on your left and down the walkway to your left will be a Renowned Hero's Soul
    • Continue circling around to the walkway on the other side to grab another Renowned Hero's Soul
    • Open the gate at the end of the walkway
    • Turn around and go back to the room with the double doors and open them. Go down the stairs and and enter the tower at the end. Head up the tower and head across the walkway to a door. Open the door to a hallway where at the end is a Silver Catalyst
    • Head back down the tower and pass through the fog gate. Pull the lever to deactivate the Ballista. Head across the walkway to pick up a Storied Soldier's Soul, the Clever Cat's Ring, a Renowned Soldier's Soul, another Renowned Soldier's Soul, and a Stone of Ephemeral Eyes
    • Turn around and just behind the arrow contraption to your right will be a tower. Head to the top and take a right. At the end of the walkway will be a door you can open to room with a Dregling you can talk to who plays innocent. Kill him so he will not be able to help the boss later
    • On the wall here is the Prison of Hope, Special Key
    • Outside, head back down the walkway and to your right will be a broken fence. Drop down onto the arch to pick up Widow's Lotus x2
    • Drop down to the ground. You will be invaded by the Baby's Nail Black Phantom. Kill her and she will drop a Black Eye Stone + Chunk of Moonlightstone x3 + Stone of Ephemeral Eyes x2
    • Make your way back to the prison and free Sage Freke, the Visionary. Talk to him to receive Geri's Stiletto and to have him move to the Nexus
    • Return to where you fought the Black Phantom and approach the massive doors for a cutscene. Pass through the fog gate and in the cathedral to your left behind you in the corner will be Old Spice x2
    • On the right side behind the third pillar will be a Storied Soldier's Soul
    • Defeat Fool's Idol
    • Pick up a Baby's Nail behind the altar, hugging the walls to avoid proceeding for now
    • Approach the altar directly to trigger a cutscene. Once you are transported by the Gargoyles, touch the Fool's Idol Archstone to acquire the Doll Demon's Soul
    • Touch the Archstone to return to the Nexus and take care of any business
    • Speak to the Crestfallen Warrior
    • Talk to Stockpile Thomas
    • Speak to the Worshipper of God
    • Talk to Freke's Apprentice
    • Speak to Sage Freke, the Visionary, who can teach you Spells if you have at least 10 Magic
    • Travel back to the Fool's Idol Archstone and suicide here to return to Soul Form

    Valley of Defilement

    • Touch the Archstone of the Chieftain and warp to the Valley of Defilement
    • Immediately check under the wooden ramp for Royal Lotus x4
    • Climb the wooden ramps until you reach the top of the structure. At the top on the right on a ledge will be a Renowned Hero's Soul
    • Drop down and stick to the right walkway. Drop down to the left of the falling boulders to pick up a Morning Star
    • Drop down a level and interact with the bridge to lower it
    • Head across to pick up a Talisman of God + Widow's Lotus x2
    • Drop down here and walk down a slope to grab an Unknown Hero's Soul
    • Go back up and cross the bridge to a platform. Around the middle of the edge here you can drop down to the lower level and pick up Crescent Moon Grass x3 and a Renowned Hero's Soul
    • Drop down again through a hole here to pick up Crescent Moon Grass x2
    • Drop down another a level to find the Ring of Magical Dullness
    • Drop down again and descend the ladder to walk out on the overhanging plank. Carefully drop down into the haystack to avoid taking fall damage. Pick up a Stone of Ephemeral Eyes here
    • Head forward and quickly kill the Crystal Lizard for upgrade materials
    • You can also pick up two Storied Soldier's Souls here
    • Ahead by the Giant Tick is a corpse with a Blessed Mace+1
    • Kick the bridge down here and pass through the fog gate. On your right will be Crescent Moon Grass
    • Head up the ramp to your left a bit to find the Filthy Woman
    • Continue up and head straight out on the ledge to pick up the Saint's Set + Stone of Ephemeral Eyes
    • Go back and head to the right across the bridge into a room with a Shard of Faintstone and an Unknown Hero's Soul in the hay
    • Climb up to the top floor to find a corpse in the corner with Royal Lotus x3
    • Drop down to the hay again, cross the bridge you kicked down earlier, and cross the walkway to the Giant Depraved One. Make your way up and cross another bridge with another Giant Depraved One. Descend the ramps here and at the bottom under the ramp will be Full Moon Grass x3
    • Pass through the fog gate. Defeat Leechmonger; acquire Wriggling Demon's Soul
    • In this area you can pick up five Crescent Moon Grass x2, two Crescent Moon Grass x5, Shard of Faintstone x2, Chunk of Faintstone, and a Great Club
    • Behind some planks here is Shard of Suckerstone x2
    • Touch the Archstone to return to the Nexus and take care of any business
    • Return to the Valley of Defilement and suicide here to return to Soul Form

    Shrine of Storms

    • Touch the Archstone of the Shadowmen and warp to the Shrine of Storms
    • Head forward under the arch and to your left you can pick up Half Moon Grass x5
    • Continue up the stairs and to your left at the top will be a Renowned Hero's Soul
    • Further to the left in the corner will be Soldier's Lotus x3
    • On the right side on a corpse against the wall will be a Talisman of God
    • Pass through the fog gate and head left through the tunnel. Take a left outside and to your right behind a tree will be Soul Remains x6
    • On the ledge behind the enemy will be a Crescent Falchion+1
    • Drop down and make your way make into the tunnel we just passed through. Head up to the rampart and at the end down the stairs will be the Copper Key
    • Go back across the rampart and head through the broken piece of wall. Continue through the tunnel with the Silver Skeleton and make your way up the stairs and out the door on your right outside. Up the first part will be an Augite of Guidance
    • Here you will also find Sparkly the Crow. Give it your spare Talisman of God
    • Reload the game and you will find that Sparkly has left you a Colorless Demon's Soul

    FAQ

    The foundation of this guide was based on Zachary Kjellberg's Dark Souls 3 Cheat Sheet code for use with IGN's Walkthrough Guide and xHolmesWalkthroughsx7's Demon's Souls Walkthrough

    Is this a guide?

    Not really. This is a set of checklists and information that you can use while playing Demon's Souls to make sure you don't miss an item, action, conversation, or boss. If you need a walkthrough, you can click on the zone titles to read the wiki walkthrough

    Does this playthrough need to be followed in this exact order?

    Demon's Souls is not a linear game and has multiple ways of progressing. This guide highlights zones as they become available, so feel free to follow however you please. You can always check things off in a different order

    Can this be used for multiple characters?

    Yes. Use the profile selector and buttons in the Options tab at the top of the page to setup multiple profiles

    How does the checklist status get saved?

    The checklist is saved to your browser's local storage. Be careful when clearing your browser's cache as it will also destroy your saved progress
